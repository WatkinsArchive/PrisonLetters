# Ian Watkins — Letters from Prison (Transcribed Archive)

A transcribed, annotated archive of letters, cards and other material Ian Watkins sent from
HMP Wakefield between his imprisonment in December 2013 and his death on 11 October 2025.

> **Content warning:** this material concerns a convicted child sex offender and includes
> sexual content and references to violence.

## What this is

Most of these letters were only ever published as photos, crops or short quotes in the
press. This version:

- **transcribes** every letter in full, as far as the image allows;
- adds a short **summary** and **background** for each item, with its date and source;
- recovers **seven reply sheets** from The Sun's video interview with his fiancée, which
  has since been deleted — some stitched together from several video frames;
- places **related items side by side**: a card's front with its inside, a full letter
  with the press excerpt or quote taken from it.

**53 items** in total:

| Section | Items |
|---|---|
| To "Ellie", his fiancée (2013–2025) | 20 |
| Ellie: visitor approval and Watkins' belongings | 6 |
| To "Minx" (about 2017) | 14 |
| Voice message from a hidden prison phone (2016) | 1 |
| Watkins' canaries in prison (2017) | 12 |

## Viewing

Open `Ian Watkins prison letters - transcribed.html` in any browser. It is a single,
self-contained file — every image and the voice-message video are embedded, so no internet
connection is required.

PNG copies of the letters recovered from the video interview are in the
`interview letters` folder.

## Features

- **Gallery** — thumbnails grouped by recipient, with dates.
- **Viewer** — click any thumbnail to open the full-size image or video alongside its
  transcription, summary, context and source. Use the arrow keys or Prev / Next to move
  between items.
- **Voice message** — the 2016 recording is playable, with the sung lines marked in its
  transcript.

## Transcription conventions

- Watkins writes in all-caps print with a "Z"-shaped capital I; this is normalised to
  ordinary sentence case. His spelling and slang are kept as written.
- **[redacted]** marks text blacked out or blurred in the source image.
- **[crossed out]** marks his own scribbles.
- **[?]** marks an uncertain reading; **…** marks text cut off or hidden in the image.
- Letters shown in the video with their left edge cut off have the missing letters filled
  in in **[square brackets]** where the reading is certain.
- Song lyrics he quotes are not reproduced; they appear as
  **[lyrics of "Title" by Artist]**.

## Censorship

This is a **censored** copy, intended for public reference:

- the two women are identified only by aliases — "Ellie" and "Minx";
- a man in Ellie's life who is named in several letters appears as **[name]** in the
  transcripts and is blurred in the images;
- Ellie's name, address and passport photos are redacted in the prison documents;
- no source file names are included, and the handle of the Twitter account the canary
  photos were posted from is deliberately left out.

## Sources

- **The Sun**, 18 October 2025 — letters to Ellie, and its video interview with her
  (since deleted).
- **The Sun Online**, 19 August 2019 — letters to Minx.
- **Reddit** — reply sheets shared by Ellie herself as "Darlablessed"; the visitor-approval
  documents and belongings photos were provided by Ellie herself through Reddit DMs. One
  letter to Minx was shared by her as her Reddit profile banner.
- **Twitter** — the canary photos and captions, posted in August 2017 from an account
  Watkins ran from a phone hidden in his cell.

## Accuracy & limitations

- Several letters survive only in part — cropped by the press, blurred before sharing, or
  partly hidden under other sheets in the video. Only the visible text is transcribed.
- Letters marked "Quoted in The Sun" have only ever been published as text; their images
  are screenshots of the article passages.
- Dates are as written on the letters where shown; otherwise they are estimated from the
  content and marked as such. Canary photo dates are when each photo was posted.
- The recipient of the 2016 voice message is not known.
